5.3 · PROPOSED EXPERIMENTS

Experiments D and C

D · V4.1 Engram ablation

Purpose: test the knowledge-store claim on real weights, with Traditional Chinese.

Method: full model vs gate = 0; facts, reading, math, code; e.g. TTQA and TMMLU+; same items in Traditional and Simplified.

Metrics: retention per category, Traditional vs Simplified gap, mean gate.

Decision: if Traditional differs clearly, don’t assume equal benefit.

C · Lookup latency

Purpose: add latency and HBM vs DRAM data the paper lacks.

Method: standalone lookup bench; 10⁵ rows up to memory limit; HBM, DRAM sync, DRAM async; uniform vs real hash traces.

Metrics: p50/p99, tokens/s, PCIe traffic, cache and TLB misses.

Decision: if real traces beat uniform on p99, tiered caching is worth building.

D can’t prove causation, since V4.1 was trained with Engram. It is still the cheapest way to see whether facts and math collapse together and whether Traditional Chinese behaves differently.
