Parameters vs FLOPs: why lookup is nearly free

P_sparse = P_tot − P_act

P_MoE = ρ · P_sparse
P_Engram = (1 − ρ) · P_sparse

  • P_tot: all trainable parameters, without embeddings and LM head
  • P_act: parameters used per token, which sets FLOPs
  • P_sparse: the “free” budget that adds capacity without compute
