3.4 · VERIFIED VS UNVERIFIED

Verified results vs unverified claims

Measured by the paper

  • Under 3% throughput loss, 100B table in DRAM
  • Iso-parameter, iso-FLOPs benchmark gains
  • Layer-placement sweep and ablations
  • Effective depth (CKA, LogitLens)

Not yet verified

  • Offload cost on an MoE backbone
  • Two modules, as in the real model
  • p50/p99 and first-token latency
  • Direct HBM vs DRAM comparison
  • NVMe tiering and real cache hit rates

Also: all gains are single reported values, and we found no complete independent reproduction.

The throughput test deliberately used dense backbones to avoid expert-parallel communication, so it differs from the production setup. The paper’s line that a fully optimized system would be negligible is phrased as “strongly suggests”.
