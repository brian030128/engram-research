The two questions the paper answers

1

Can a Transformer get a native lookup, so static patterns are retrieved instead of computed?

2

With total parameters fixed, how much should go to experts and how much to memory?

Question 1 produced the Engram module, conditional memory. Question 2 is the paper’s sparsity-allocation problem; the answer is most of the spare budget to MoE, roughly 20 to 25 percent to Engram.
