At a glance: five judgments

1 · The architecture works and ships. It beats an iso-parameter, iso-FLOPs MoE on all 27 benchmarks and is used in DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash.

2 · Gains are steady but moderate. Median +2.5 points with no error bars; going from Dense to MoE gives a median +9.5.

3 · “Main store of facts” is weakly supported. Removing Engram hurts math about as much as factual recall.

4 · It is not a RAG replacement. No source citations, no update path without training, and keys of only 2–4 tokens.

5 · Traditional Chinese is untested. None of 2,171 Traditional/Simplified token pairs is merged by compression.

Lead with the conclusions; the rest of the deck supports each one. Judgment 1 is the paper’s strongest result; 3 and 5 are where our own analysis adds something new.
