2 · EXPERIMENT A

Collisions are the norm; frequency decides who wins

Hash quality is good: across 60 settings, measured rates are within 0.12 points of random-hash theory.

At real scale: 3-gram types likely far exceed rows, so nearly every n-gram collides on every head (rough extrapolation).

Under heavy load: 3-grams seen 100+ times top 7.2 of their 8 rows on average; those seen once top none.

Our measurement on 53.6M tokens of English and Chinese Wikipedia and Bitext support data. Text statistics only, not trained representation quality.

The paper gives no quantitative collision analysis, so we ran one. Sharing one mix value across heads caused no detectable correlation. Implication: rare domain terms struggle to get their own rows when the table is trained on general text.
