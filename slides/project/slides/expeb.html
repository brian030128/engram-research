5 · EXPERIMENT DESIGN

Experiments E and B

E · Frozen-backbone updates

Purpose: answer “can memory be updated alone?” against RAG and LoRA.

Method: fictional support FAQ; trained vs unseen phrasings; table-only training vs LoRA vs RAG.

Metrics: accuracy on trained and reworded questions, cross-script accuracy, drift (KL), update time and size.

Decision: only worth productizing if reworded questions approach RAG.

B · Small-scale allocation

Purpose: check the U-shape at a scale we can afford.

Method: small MoE at fixed total and active parameters; MoE share 100, 90, 80, 70, 50, 40%; 3 seeds each.

Metrics: val loss mean ± std, key benchmarks, training tokens/s.

Decision: reproduced only if the gain at 80% exceeds seed-to-seed spread.

For E, fictional facts make sure the model couldn’t already know them. For B, a small scale may not show the U-shape at all, which would itself be useful to know.
