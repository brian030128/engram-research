The problem: models compute what they could look up

LayerWhat the model makes of “Wales”
1–2A region of the United Kingdom
3A place in Europe
4–5The title “Princess of Wales”
6Diana, Princess of Wales herself

A fixed fact, the same every time, rebuilt on every pass.

With no lookup primitive, a Transformer assembles entities over several layers of attention and FFN.

Paper Table 3, reproduced from Ghandeharioun et al. (Patchscopes): the model’s reading of the last token in “Diana, Princess of Wales”.

The paper calls this simulating retrieval through computation. Those early layers could be spent on reasoning instead.
