Our synthesis from each technique’s design; the paper does not compare against RAG or LoRA.

Both Engram and RAG are lookups, but at different levels: Engram returns an unreadable vector per token inside the model; RAG returns readable, citable text before generation. They may complement each other, since Engram improves long-context use, but that combination is untested.
